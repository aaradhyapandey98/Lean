The purpose of this project is to explore the space of equational theories of magmas, ordered by implication. To begin with, we focus on theories of a single equation, and specifically on a finite list of equational laws up to symmetry and relabeling.

We accumulate both proven and conjectured implications and anti-implications. Proven assertions are verified in a proof assistant, while conjectured assertions represent claims not yet verified.

Examples

  • Equation 1: x = x. The trivial law.
  • Equation 2: x = y. The singleton law.
  • Equation 43: x ◇ y = y ◇ x. The commutative law.
  • Equation 46: x ◇ y = z ◇ w. The constant law.
  • Equation 4512: x ◇ (y ◇ z) = (x ◇ y) ◇ z. The associative law.
